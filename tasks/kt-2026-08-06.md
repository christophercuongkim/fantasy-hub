## Audit of `/hall_of_records` vs DS rules (this session)
Ranked findings — **V2 already fixed**:
- **V1 (a11y, top priority):** `FinishHeatmap`/`H2HHeatmap`/`LuckSkill` are
  pointer-only — no `tabIndex`/`onFocus`/`aria`; H2H win-rate is tooltip-only (no
  in-cell text) → unreachable by keyboard, unreliable on touch. 0016 says
  pointer-only is non-conformant. `BumpChart` is the reference to copy.
- **V2 ✅ fixed:** `rx={2}` → `rx={0}` on heatmap cells (DS is square-corners-only).
- **V3 (type):** h1/h2/caption hand-compose raw sizes (`var(--text-4xl) …`) instead
  of composed `--type-title`/`--type-heading`/`--type-caption` roles.
- **V4 (motion):** `globals.css` `sl-*` animations hardcode `0.9s`/`0.5s` + bare
  `ease-out`; should use `--dur-*`/`--ease-*`; `0.9s` > `--dur-slow` (320ms).
  (reduced-motion IS honored.)
- **V5 (type/charts):** SVG tick/cell numbers are sans `fontSize="9"` (below the
  10px `--text-2xs` floor, not mono/tabular). Award figures already do it right.
- **Uncertain:** U1 tables have no interactive `sort`; U3 scatter paints ~half the
  points accent (lucky/unlucky 2-way — one hue, borderline); U5 Career/Champions
  declare no `secondary` col so the `sm` list-row 2nd line is empty.
- **Conformant:** colors fully tokenized; `--chart-seq` magnitude w/ live ink-flip;
  single accent; the mandatory >6-lines adjacent grid (bump + finish heatmap);
  required cell hairlines; DS `Table` w/ full responsive metadata + `caption`; flat
  cards; award figures mono-tabular; spacing on `--space-*`; touch pin-filter.
